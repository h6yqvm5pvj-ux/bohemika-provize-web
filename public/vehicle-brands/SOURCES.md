# Vehicle brand logos

SVGs from [Simple Icons 11.15.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/11.15.0/icons), downloaded from the versioned npm distribution at `https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@11.15.0/icons/SLUG.svg`.

Simple Icons distributes these assets under [CC0 1.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/11.15.0/LICENSE.md). Brand names and marks belong to their respective owners. The marks identify the manufacturer of the vehicle returned by the registry; they do not indicate a partnership or endorsement.

The SVG files are served locally, without contacting an external image service during a vehicle lookup.
