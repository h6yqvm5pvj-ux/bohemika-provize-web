## Finální zadání pro imagegen

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a brand new modern 3D animation-style trophy icon for a polished business app. A simple, beautifully proportioned award cup with thick rounded handles and a broad smooth blank front, a short stem, and a low softly rounded lavender pedestal. Clean art-directed CGI resembling an object in a high-end animated motion-design film: soft sculpted geometry, matte satin champagne-gold material with subtle tonal variation, pale lavender and ivory details, very soft studio lighting and gentle ambient occlusion, crisp clean silhouette. No photorealistic metal, no mirror reflections, no scratches, no intricate decoration, no sparkles, no floating confetti, no text, no logos or markings; leave the cup front completely blank for a brand emblem to be added in the app. Front-facing camera with a slight view from above, symmetrical composition, the entire object visible, centered with generous empty margins. Premium restrained friendly 3D illustration, not a realistic sports cup, no dramatic shine, no AI fantasy details. The object must be isolated on a truly transparent background with real PNG alpha. No ground plane, scenery, background panel, colors or patterns. Square image.
